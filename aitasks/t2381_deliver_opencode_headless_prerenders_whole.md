## Origin

Spawned from t2365 during Step 8b review.

## Upstream defect

- `.aitask-scripts/aitask_setup.sh:5275-5281 — setup_opencode copies only SKILL.md of every staged aitask-* dir, so the rendered aitask-pickrem-remote-/aitask-pickweb-remote- prerenders are half-installed (no task-workflow-remote-/ait-git-remote- closure) and opencode_tool_mapping.md is overwritten unconditionally — the OpenCode twin of this task's Codex defect`

## Diagnostic context

t2365 fixed the same shape for Codex. Before it, `install.sh` only staged `codex_skills/` into `aitasks/metadata/codex_skills/`, and `setup_codex_cli` copied `aitask-*/SKILL.md` alone. The committed remote prerenders therefore went stale on upgrade, and their closure never reached a consumer checkout. OpenCode still follows that pre-fix path:
- `install_opencode_staging` stages `opencode_skills/` only;
- `setup_opencode` copies `SKILL.md` of each `aitask-*` dir (rendered `-remote-` dirs included) and nothing else;
- it overwrites `opencode_tool_mapping.md` / `opencode_planmode_prereqs.md` with no check for local changes.

t2365 built the pieces to reuse:
- `seed/headless_prerenders.txt` already lists the OpenCode files (hash and git mode), generated by `aitask_release_verify.sh` / `aitask_headless_manifest.sh` on the clean release checkout;
- `.aitask-scripts/lib/headless_prerenders.sh` has the ignore-independent classifier (`ait_manifest_changes`, strict/bootstrap modes), the staging-verified no-follow publish, the data-root resolver use, and the rejected-manifest handling;
- `tests/test_headless_prerender_delivery.sh` and `tests/lib/headless_release_fixture.sh` hold the fixtures (fresh install, first setup bootstrap, upgrade, branch-mode data worktree, preservation cases).

The classifier and sync currently consider only `.agents/skills/` paths, by design (t2365's Codex-only scope).

## Suggested fix

Generalize the delivery to `.opencode/skills/` manifest paths: classify, sync from `aitasks/metadata/opencode_skills/`, and baseline-protect. In `setup_opencode`, skip rendered `*-` dirs and let the sync own the mapping files when a manifest is stored. Mirror t2365's I0/B1/B2/U1/DB1 cases for `.opencode/`.
